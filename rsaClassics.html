Discover the rich culinary heritage of South Africa with our selection of classic dishes, each crafted to bring out the authentic flavours and traditions of the region.

Fish and Chips

Enjoy crispy, golden fish served with thick-cut chips, a beloved classic that brings a taste of the coast to your plate.

Comes with two fish options (hake or snoek) and a choice of slap chips, yellow rice or green salad.

Peri-Peri Chicken

Experience the fiery heat of our Peri-Peri chicken, a spicy delight that showcases the bold flavours of South African cuisine.

Served with a choice of slap chips, yellow rice or green salad.

Braai

Indulge in the smoky, savoury flavours of our Braai, a traditional South African barbecue that brings people together around the fire.

Served with pap, chakalaka, salsa, four chicken wings, 125g boerewors, and a protein of choice (beef T-bone, pork chop or lamb chop).
