Quick Snack Streetwise

Experience the best of South African street food with our quick and delicious sandwiches, perfect for a fast meal on the go.

Kota

Enjoy the classic South African Kota, a hearty sandwich filled with slap chips, russian, egg, polony, vienna, and flavourful sauces.

Gatsby

Indulge in our Gatsby, a long sandwich packed with a variety of fillings, perfect for sharing or enjoying on your own.

Served with a variety of proteins (beef masala, chicken masala or mutton masala).

Amagwinya

Try our Amagwinya, a popular South African snack made from deep-fried dough, perfect for a quick bite.

Served with a variety of fillings (mince curry, chicken livers, or polony & cheese with russian).

Boerewors Roll

Enjoy our Boerewors Roll, a traditional South African sausage served in a roll with your choice of caramelised onions or tomato relish.

Our Menu

We offer a variety of dishes designed to be both quick and delicious. From classic South African comfort foods to innovative culinary creations, our menu has something for everyone.

Plate Kos

Experience the bold flavours of South African cuisine with our signature Plate Kos — hearty dishes that combine traditional ingredients with a modern twist.

Seven Colours Plate

Indulge in the vibrant flavours of our Seven Colours dish, a celebration of South African ingredients presented in a visually stunning array.

Offered with any protein of your choice (chicken, beef or mutton), yellow or plain rice, and a choice of three vegetable sides.

Durban Curry

Spice up your meal with our Durban Curry, a rich and aromatic dish that captures the essence of South African Indian cuisine.

Served with naan bread and a protein of your choice (chicken, beef or mutton).

Bobotie

Enjoy the rich and savoury flavours of our Bobotie, a traditional South African dish combining spiced meat with a creamy topping.
